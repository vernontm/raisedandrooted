Questions, Answered

What parents ask us first

What exactly is a "micro school"?
A micro school is a small, intentionally-limited learning community, think of the best parts of a one-room schoolhouse, modernized. Far fewer students per teacher than a traditional school, close-knit groups, and a curriculum built around mastery and real-world skills rather than standardized pacing.
What ages and grades do you serve?
We serve grades K–8 here in Katy, TX. If you're unsure whether your child is a fit, the best next step is to grab the free guide and reach out, we'll talk through your child specifically.
How much does it cost?
Tuition and any available family options are shared during enrollment conversations. Because spots are limited, we keep this personal, start with the free guide and we'll walk you through everything.
Is this academically rigorous, or just "feel-good"?
Both, on purpose. Small groups let us push each child harder and support them better than a class of thirty ever could. Rigor and being truly known are not opposites, kids do their best work when they feel safe and seen.
We're not sure we're ready to enroll. Is the guide still worth it?
Absolutely. The guide is built to help any parent make a better school decision, even if it's not with us. Start there, no pressure.
